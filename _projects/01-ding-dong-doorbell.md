A QR code doorbell. The quickest and easiest way to get a doorbell for just about anywhere. You download the app, generate a QR code and put it where you want a doorbell. This project grew from a personal need and graduated to the mobile stores.

[Get your QR code doorbell](https://dingdongdoorbell.com)
