---
title: Less (about) Tech
---

One of my approaches is “Less Tech”, or “Less about Tech”.

**“Less about Tech”** emphasizes that technology stands in service of humans. It’s not primarily about the tech, the tech is a tool that helps achieve a goal. The important question is always what are we trying to do and for whom? It’s typically only at the “how” where tech enters the conversation.

But it’s also about **“Less Tech”**. Technology costs money to develop, maintain and support. It tends to create complexity and can lead to inflexibility. When pursuing a goal, you have to ask - will a solution with more tech be cheaper in the long run compared to a suboptimal solution with less tech?
